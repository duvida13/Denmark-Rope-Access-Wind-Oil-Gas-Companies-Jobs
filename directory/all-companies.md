# Denmark company directory

**103 company/group records — researched 16 November 2026.**

Danish companies and international companies with Danish operations across rope access, wind, and oil and gas. Small trade contractors and specialist blade/service businesses are included alongside manufacturers, operators, staffing firms and offshore construction companies. A directory entry does not imply a current vacancy or eligibility for every role.

One master table; overlapping sectors are tagged on the same record. Related brands are consolidated where appropriate. Altrad's industrial-services and Alpha wind businesses are listed separately because their service and recruitment routes differ, with their shared group made explicit.

Website/evidence, careers and contact links below are the public sources used. Sources were reviewed through web retrieval/search on the date above; a link being listed is not a claim that an application was submitted or that every linked form was tested. Unknowns and failed retrievals are stated.
