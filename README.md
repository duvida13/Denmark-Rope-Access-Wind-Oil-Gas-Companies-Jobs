**103 researched company/group records · Updated 16 November 2026**

Danish company and recruitment research, including small contractors, specialist subcontractors and agencies alongside major wind and offshore employers.

## Scope

Danish companies and international companies with Danish operations. Includes industrial and building-maintenance rope access, wind installation/service/blade repair, oil and gas maintenance, rigging, scaffolding, insulation, coatings, inspection, staffing and relevant offshore construction.

Companies can be worth approaching without an advertised vacancy. Overlapping sectors are marked once per company; legacy brands and group relationships are explained.

## Research status

Expanded from 51 to 103 records on 16 November 2026, with 52 additional companies covering local rope access, wind service, offshore trades staffing, lifting, fabrication and equipment suppliers.

The directory is populated from company sources, Danish/English searches, the official wind-service register, regional supplier networks and historical GitHub findings. It is an exploratory directory, not an exhaustive census or a claim that all companies are hiring.

Personal LinkedIn connections remain unchecked because the available browser session requires sign-in. Public company pages were used where accessible. Remaining small-company leads are retained in the progress notes.

Uses the same directory / jobs / context layout as the [Swiss research repository](https://github.com/duvida13/Switzerland-Rope-Access-Companies-Jobs), adapted to Denmark's broader sector scope.

Updates are on request. No scheduled research, outreach or applications are configured. Keep CVs, credentials and private correspondence outside this public repository.
