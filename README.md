# Denmark — Rope Access, Wind, Oil & Gas

Company and recruitment research covering Denmark, including small contractors, specialist subcontractors and agencies.

## Open the research

- [Company directory](directory/all-companies.md) — one master table for company details, contacts and sources.
- [Job findings](jobs/findings.md) — separately verified vacancies.
- [Research methodology](context/methodology.md) — multiple discovery approaches across all three sectors.
- [Progress and next checks](context/handoff.md).

## Scope

Danish companies and international companies with Danish operations working in rope access, wind turbines / wind energy, and oil and gas. Include companies worth approaching even when they have no advertised vacancy. Record overlapping sectors once per company.

Repository setup completed on **16 November 2026**. Company research and vacancy verification are pending; no findings are claimed yet.
