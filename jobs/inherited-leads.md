# Historical Danish job leads

Extracted on **16 November 2026** from [Combined Rope Access Job Findings — vacancies.csv](https://github.com/duvida13/Combined-Rope-Access-Job-Findings/blob/main/vacancies.csv).

**73 Denmark-labelled records.** These are historical discovery records, not 73 current vacancies, unique employers or independently verified advertisements. Original links and source status are preserved; short Indeed links and anonymous boards are not assumed to identify an employer. The main directory contains companies rechecked against public company sources.

The combined repository's [source checkpoints](https://github.com/duvida13/Combined-Rope-Access-Job-Findings/blob/main/state/source-checkpoints.json) record a 16 November merge of the ChatGPT, Claude and Grok sources. This review used that consolidated history, plus the GROK-BOT employer notes and latest accessible source digests; it did not independently re-open every historical source file or every advertisement.

Companies traced from this history include Davai, Rebteknikeren, Sky-Work, RT9, Semco, SubC, Bilfinger, Altrad, Global Wind Service, Swire, Vento, Vestas, RWE and Industrial Climbing Solutions. Danish Blade Service is now retained under Clobotics. The Grok employer notes additionally surfaced Flex Wind, Muehlhan, Sky-Energy and QWC.
