## Coverage and limits

103 is the current researched directory, not a market census. Presence in the directory does not establish current hiring, English-only acceptance, a particular rotation, Danish deployment for every role, or acceptance of the user's current certifications.

Individual records distinguish job portals, unsolicited applications and general business enquiry routes. Do not send applications to commercial addresses when a company requires its recruitment portal.

Training-only providers are not mixed into the company employment directory. Member/supplier directories remain discovery sources rather than proof of hiring.
