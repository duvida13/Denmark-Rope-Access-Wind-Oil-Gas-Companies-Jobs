# Denmark research progress

**Research date: 16 November 2026.**

## Completed

- Published 51 company/group records in [the master directory](../directory/all-companies.md), covering rope access, wind, oil and gas, technical staffing and offshore construction.
- Included small commercial-climbing contractors and blade/service specialists as well as major employers; no company-size cutoff.
- Used Danish/English searches, primary company service/contact/career pages, Denmark's official wind service-company register, DWP regional supplier network and Business Esbjerg's offshore manpower guide.
- Reviewed all 4,125 rows of the merged vacancies index for Denmark-labelled records: 73 extracted and preserved in [historical leads](../jobs/inherited-leads.md). Reviewed source checkpoints, GROK-BOT employer notes, 16 November ChatGPT/Grok digests and the latest available 15 November Claude digest. This is consolidated-history coverage, not a claim that every historical source digest or all 73 URLs were independently re-opened.
- Rechecked company identities and useful recruitment routes; traced legacy Danish Blade Service to Clobotics, Alpha Offshore to Altrad Alpha, and current Vento/Flex Wind routes.
- Added three specific employer advertisements with visible apply routes and eleven general application intakes in [recruitment findings](../jobs/findings.md). Identified closed, aging, expired and unavailable examples separately.
- No applications or messages sent; source repositories were not changed.

## Coverage limits

This is a substantial first research pass, not a census of every Danish employer. Company inclusion means service/presence evidence was found, not that a vacancy, English-only work, suitable rotation or acceptance of existing qualifications is guaranteed. Some contact routes are general business enquiries rather than recruiting inboxes. Career and address discrepancies are recorded in the master table.

Further work can expand IRATA's live member directory, energy-cluster/exhibitor lists, local port supply chains and current small-company contacts. Training-only providers have not been mixed into the employment directory.
