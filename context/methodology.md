# Denmark research methodology

## Coverage

Search across rope access, wind energy, and oil and gas. Include Danish employers and international companies with Danish operations, small companies, subcontractors and specialist recruiters. Keep one master company record with multiple sector tags. No suitability rankings.

## Discovery approaches

1. Search company services and careers pages in Danish and English. Include industriklatring, industriklatrer, erhvervsklatring, rope access, vindmølleservice, vindmølletekniker, vingereparation, offshore vedligehold, olie og gas, stillads, isolering and rigging.
2. Check IRATA member directories and relevant Danish trade and industry associations. Membership is a discovery source, not proof of current hiring.
3. Trace wind manufacturers, installation and service contractors, blade repair specialists, commissioning teams and their supplier networks.
4. Trace oil and gas operators, drilling contractors, offshore maintenance, inspection/NDT, coatings, rigging, scaffolding and insulation subcontractors.
5. Search port business directories, regional networks and industry exhibitor/member lists, including Esbjerg and other Danish energy and industrial hubs. Deliberately look for small local firms.
6. Review all accessible relevant GitHub job-finding repositories for Danish companies, recruiters and advertisers. Preserve source links; historical findings do not establish current vacancies.
7. Review accessible LinkedIn company pages, recruiter posts and the user's Danish professional connections through an authorized logged-in browser. Do not claim private connections were checked unless accessed.
8. Follow project announcements, contractor partnerships, training-provider deployment activity and supplier references to uncover companies missed by direct searches.

## Verification and recording

Verify each company's services and Danish presence using primary sources where possible. Record name, sectors, relevant work, Danish location/operations, website, careers route, public business contacts, source URLs, checked date and unresolved points. Distinguish direct delivery, staffing and training.

Deduplicate brands, legal entities and acquisitions carefully; retain aliases when useful. Do not infer an email address, hiring status, English-language acceptance or certification equivalence.

Companies need not advertise a current vacancy to qualify. Verify vacancy status separately, checking deadlines, closure notices, broken links and the live application route.

## Workflow

GitHub is the working research record. Updates are on request. Sending messages and applications requires a separate user instruction. Keep CVs, private correspondence and credentials outside this public repository.
